## Project Overview
This project presents a **comprehensive Revenue Analysis Dashboard** built using **Power BI**, designed to monitor financial performance, sales distribution, customer behavior, and sales representative productivity across multiple regions and product categories.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to quickly identify revenue drivers, cost inefficiencies, and regional performance gaps through interactive visuals and KPIs.

---

## Executive Summary
Despite generating **high total revenue (59.69M)**, the business is currently operating at a **net loss of -4.16M**, resulting in a **negative profit margin of -6.96%**.  
This indicates that operational or cost-related inefficiencies are outweighing sales performance.

Key revenue contributions come from **New Customers** and **Online Sales Channels**, suggesting strong market acquisition but a need for improved cost control and profitability strategies.

## Key Insights
- **New Customers** are the largest contributors to total revenue, indicating effective acquisition strategies.
- **Online Sales Channels** slightly outperform retail, highlighting a shift toward digital purchasing behavior.
- **Northern Region** leads in overall revenue, with strong individual sales rep performance.
- High costs are eroding profitability, making **cost optimization** a critical focus area.
